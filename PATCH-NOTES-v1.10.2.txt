Survivor League v1.10.2

Scoring corrections
- Reconciles a live character's vanilla kill counter with Survivor League's current streak.
- Preserves kills from previous lives and adds previously missed current-life baseline kills to lifetime totals.
- Does not add baseline corrections to the active season score.
- Stabilizes after reconciliation so later client reports and restarts do not double-count kills.
- Best streak retains the highest verified current-life counter.
- Admin score correction now permits a current streak greater than the current season score when the streak spans a season boundary.

Examples from the read-only recovery export
- Karma: lifetime/streak 1/1 with live baseline 2 becomes 2/2.
- GhostMachine: lifetime/streak 305/231 with live baseline 475 becomes 549/475.
- Bisk: lifetime/streak 1199/1199 with live baseline 2484 becomes 2484/2484.
